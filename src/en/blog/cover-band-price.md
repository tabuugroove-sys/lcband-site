“How much does a cover band cost?” is the first question for almost every event. The honest answer is that it **depends on the lineup, duration, date and city**. Below are the formats and details used to prepare a quote.

## Formats to discuss

Start with the role of music in your event:

<div class="prose-table-wrap">

| Format | Purpose | Details needed for a quote |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acoustic set | Welcome reception or ceremony | Instruments, duration and sound setup |
| Compact lineup | Dinner or a smaller venue | Vocals, instruments and performance space |
| Main programme | Concert and dance sets | Musicians, number of sets, breaks and songs |
| Extended lineup | Additional musicians | Lineup, stage, technical rider and rehearsals |

</div>

We confirm the price after checking the date, musicians and venue. Welcome sets, extra blocks, a DJ, equipment and travel are listed in the proposal when requested.

## What changes the price

**Lineup.** This is the main factor. More [singers]({{ '/en/vocalists/' | url }}) and instruments create a fuller sound and broader repertoire, but they also cost more. The best lineup is often the one matched precisely to the venue rather than the largest possible band.

**Duration.** The standard is two 45-minute sets or three 30-minute sets. A welcome set, extra blocks and work into the early hours are priced separately.

**Date.** Availability and rates depend on the exact date. For December and New Year events, we confirm the lineup and booking conditions separately.

**City and logistics.** The location affects transport, accommodation and scheduling. We agree these costs for the specific venue and route.

**[Sound, lighting and stage]({{ '/en/riders/' | url }}).** We bring the band's instruments. The venue usually supplies powerful front-of-house sound, lighting and the stage; if you need us to arrange them, they appear as separate transparent lines in the quote.

**Additional options.** A bespoke song about your story, a [themed Italian, Latin or retro program]({{ '/en/programs/' | url }}), and guest interaction on stage can all be added to the core format.

## Why the lowest price is rarely the best target

Live music is a central part of the evening. When comparing proposals, check who will perform, hear that lineup, and confirm preparation, equipment and any substitution conditions.

It is still possible to stay within budget. A smaller lineup or shorter programme may fit your budget. We can discuss these options against the tasks and timing of your event.
