“How much does a cover band cost?” is the first question for almost every event. The honest answer is that it **depends on the lineup, duration, date and city**. Below are real price ranges and the factors behind them.

## Price ranges in Moscow

The quote begins with the size of the lineup, from an acoustic duo to a full band with a horn section. Current guide prices for Moscow are:

<div class="prose-table-wrap">

| Format | Lineup | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short acoustic set | Solo / duo for welcome or ceremony | from RUB 30,000 |
| Compact band | 3–4 performers | from RUB 90,000 |
| Core live lineup | 4–5 performers | RUB 140,000–180,000 |
| Extended lineup | 6–7 performers, 2–3 singers | RUB 190,000–250,000 |

</div>

These are guide prices for a standard Moscow event. The exact quote is always based on the date, venue and duration; we normally send it within a day.

## What changes the price

**Lineup.** This is the main factor. More singers and instruments create a fuller sound and broader repertoire, but they also cost more. The best lineup is often the one matched precisely to the venue rather than the largest possible band.

**Duration.** The standard is two 45-minute sets or three 30-minute sets. A welcome set, extra blocks and work into the early hours are priced separately.

**Date.** Fridays, Saturdays and especially **late December** are peak dates. New Year corporate parties have separate rates and book first. Early booking secures both the date and lineup.

**City and logistics.** Moscow and nearby venues use the base rate. Other cities and international tours include travel, accommodation and schedule costs.

**Sound, lighting and stage.** We bring the band's instruments. The venue usually supplies powerful front-of-house sound, lighting and the stage; if you need us to arrange them, they appear as separate transparent lines in the quote.

**Additional options.** A bespoke song about your story, a themed Italian, Latin or retro program, and guest interaction on stage can all be added to the core format.

## Why the lowest price is rarely the best target

Live music is a central part of the evening. A price far below the market often means a random lineup without rehearsals, weak sound or a last-minute substitution. We work with a fixed team, rehearse for the event and take responsibility for the result on stage.

It is still possible to stay within budget. A compact format at the moments that matter is more effective than booking a large lineup “just in case.” We will show you where to save without losing quality.
